How to Play

  • Find 5 hidden words on the board. They fit the theme, run across or down, and never touch.
  • Tap a letter to call it. One square with that letter lights up, shortest word first.
  • Gray key = no more of that letter.
  • Sonar +1: ping any square once a game, free.
  • After 15 calls, fill in the words. One wrong guess ends the game.
  • Hard: fill-in letters are checked as you type. One wrong letter ends the game.
  • Stars: 3 words ⭐ · 4 words ⭐⭐ · all 5 ⭐⭐⭐
